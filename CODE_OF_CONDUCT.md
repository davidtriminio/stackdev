# Code of conduct

We want stackdev to be a place where people of any background and experience level can learn and build together.

- Be respectful. Disagree with ideas, not with people.
- Assume good faith and say what you need clearly; questions from beginners are welcome.
- No harassment, insults, discrimination or personal attacks, in any language.
- Keep issues and pull requests on topic.

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, close issues and block people who break these rules. To report a problem, write privately to the maintainer through the contact details on the GitHub profile [davidtriminio](https://github.com/davidtriminio).

This text is adapted from the spirit of the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/).
